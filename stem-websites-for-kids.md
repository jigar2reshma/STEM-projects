# 30 Free STEM Websites for Kids

Source: [@ayooobami](https://www.instagram.com/ayooobami/) Instagram carousel, September 2026.
Curated for curious kids — games, videos, experiments, and lessons across science, tech, engineering, and math.

1. [NASA Kids' Club](https://www.nasa.gov/learning-resources/nasa-kids-club) — Space games, videos and activities from NASA.
2. [BrainPOP Jr.](https://jr.brainpop.com) — Animated science videos with quizzes and fun activities.
3. [NatGeo Kids](https://kids.nationalgeographic.com) — Explore science, animals, culture and the world around you.
4. [Easy Science for Kids](https://easyscienceforkids.com) — Articles, worksheets, videos and fun science quizzes.
5. [Wonderopolis](https://wonderopolis.org) — Daily answers to kids' big questions with a STEM twist.
6. [Cool Science Experiments HQ](https://coolscienceexperimentshq.com) — Simple, fun and safe experiments for kids.
7. [Science Bob](https://sciencebob.com) — Fun experiments, science fair ideas and resources.
8. [Science Fun for Everyone](https://sciencefun.org) — Experiments, trivia and resources for all ages.
9. [Science Buddies](https://www.sciencebuddies.org) — STEM projects, kits and science fair help.
10. [Science News Explores](https://www.snexplores.org) — Kid-friendly articles on current science topics.
11. [Smithsonian Science Education Center](https://ssec.si.edu) — Inquiry-based lessons and STEM resources.
12. [The Kid Should See This](https://thekidshouldseethis.com) — Curated science videos to spark curiosity.
13. [Exploratorium](https://www.exploratorium.edu) — Interactive science content on astronomy, biology and more.
14. [HowStuffWorks](https://www.howstuffworks.com) — Explains real-world science in a fun, easy way.
15. [PBS KIDS](https://pbskids.org) — Games and videos on animals, space and the natural world.
16. [Ducksters](https://www.ducksters.com) — Simple articles on science topics for kids.
17. [Scholastic Science World](https://scienceworld.scholastic.com) — Articles, quizzes and interactive science activities.
18. [Neuroscience for Kids](https://faculty.washington.edu/chudler/neurok.html) — Fun brain science with activities and facts.
19. [SciShow Kids](https://www.youtube.com/@SciShowKids) — Engaging science videos on exciting topics (YouTube channel).
20. [Tynker](https://www.tynker.com) — Fun coding games and lessons for kids.
21. [Code.org](https://code.org) — Free coding activities and STEM games.
22. [Curiosity Machine](https://www.curiositymachine.org) — Engineering challenges and AI lessons for kids.
23. [Mystery Science](https://mysteryscience.com) — Hands-on science lessons made easy.
24. [Little Bins for Little Hands](https://littlebinsforlittlehands.com) — Simple STEM projects using everyday items.
25. [Scratch](https://scratch.mit.edu) — Create stories, games and animations.
26. [The Concord Consortium](https://concord.org) — Interactive STEM simulations and games.
27. [PhET Interactive Simulations](https://phet.colorado.edu) — Explore science with interactive simulations.
28. [NASA Climate Kids](https://climatekids.nasa.gov) — Games and activities about Earth and climate.
29. [Discovery Education](https://www.discoveryeducation.com) — Videos, games and hands-on STEM activities. (No standalone "Expedition" site found — link goes to Discovery Education's main site.)
30. [ck-12](https://www.ck12.org) — Practice math & science with interactive lessons.
